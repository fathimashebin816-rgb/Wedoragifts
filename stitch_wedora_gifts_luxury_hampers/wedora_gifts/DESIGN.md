## Brand & Style

This design system embodies bespoke luxury gifting, curated matrimonial aesthetics, and romantic elegance. Crafted for affluent couples, discerning wedding guests, bridal parties, and corporate patrons seeking bespoke hamper experiences, the emotional atmosphere must feel intimate, polished, celebratory, and reassuringly tactile.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**. It eschews sterile transactional commerce in favor of an artisanal atelier atmosphere: generous breathing space, soft-diffused warmth, delicate hair-thin golden dividers, and evocative, editorial-grade typographic balance. The interface mirrors physical unboxing ceremonies—the whispering rustle of tissue paper, crisp satin grosgrain ribbons, embossed foil seals, and bespoke parchment notes.

## Colors

The palette references bridal silks, champagne toasts, warm alabaster, and botanical blush petals:

- **Primary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted champagne gold used for primary call-to-actions, refined active states, monogram emblems, and delicate micro-highlights.
- **Secondary (`#F7D7CE`)**: Warm blush petal, applied to soft interactive surfaces, badge backgrounds, unboxing indicators, and highlight overlays.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Radiant antique gold accent, reserved strictly for premium seals, metallic badges, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral Surface Tone (`#FAF6F0`)**: Warm ivory foundation. Backgrounds transition between `#FDFBF7` (canvas base) and `#FAF6F0` (elevated card containers).
- **Text & Contrast Tone (`#2A2421` / `#594D46`)**: Rich espresso and warm charcoal provide crisp, accessible legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Border & Accent Stroke (`#ECD5CD` / `#E8DCD1`)**: Rose-dusted whisper strokes and ivory seam borders ensure structural boundaries remain ethereal and soft.

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances the romantic, editorial gravitas of `Playfair Display` with the geometric clarity and ergonomic legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Serif Roles (`Playfair Display`)**: Applied to titles, narrative storytelling, hamper collection banners, and price totals. Headlines embrace subtle italic accents when denoting artisanal descriptors (e.g., *“The Monogrammed Vintage”*).
- **Sans-Serif Roles (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Applied across specifications, hamper ingredient inventories, configuration toggles, cart tables, and microcopy.
- **Caps & Spacing**: All uppercase labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) utilize extended kerning (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to evoke fragrance-house and couture packaging standards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a centered, 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous, relaxed negative space:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, max content container width of 1320px, 48px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutters. Product showcases feature asymmetric, museum-like catalog layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Hamper customization flows collapse into coordinated dual-column side-by-side viewports.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 20px outer margin, 16px column gutters. Full-width edge-to-edge media carousels with peek previews.

Vertical rhythm relies on wide spacing bands (`space-xl` and section gaps up to 4.5rem) to reinforce luxury pacing and eliminate transactional congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm-tinted ambient glows, luminous surface tiers, and hairline golden edges:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#FDFBF7` parchment surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Hamper Tiles)**: `#FAF6F0` surface framed with a 1px border of `#F7D7CE` (35% opacity), accompanied by an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States)**: Raised card posture with `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.1)`. The gold perimeter illuminates slightly.
- **Level 3 (Bespoke Builder Modals & Floating Carts)**: Surface tinted `#FFFFFF` with glassmorphic backdrop filter (`blur(12px) rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.85)`), bordered with hairline champagne metallic stroke, floating atop a deep shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape geometry is soft, architectural, and tailored (`roundedness: 1`). It mirrors handcrafted gift boxes, deckle-edge correspondence cards, and bespoke tags.

- Buttons and standard interactive cards adopt crisp, gentle corners (4px radius).
- Ribbon tags and boutique gift badges adopt 8px subtle corner curves.
- Wax seals, monogram medallions, and botanical icon discs remain flawless circles (9999px).
- Heavy pill capsules are explicitly avoided for buttons to retain timeless stationery proportions over casual tech tropes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#C5A059` champagne gold fill, crisp `#FAF6F0` label (`label-lg`), subtle satin finish. On hover, soft gold illumination with 2px vertical lift.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline)**: `#FAF6F0` surface, 1px border in `#C5A059`, text in `#2A2421`. On hover, fills with `#FCEEE9` blush tint.
- **Tertiary (Ghost Link)**: Understated `#2A2421` text with an animated 1px hairline underline that draws in from left to right in `#C5A059`.

### Product & Hamper Cards
- Structured ivory surfaces (`#FAF6F0`) bound by delicate rose borders (`#ECD5CD`).
- Imagery features warm-tone editorial studio photography with ample frame padding (8px inner border matting), creating a boxed gift effect.
- Features quick-reveal “Curate This Hamper” and “Custom Monogram” micro-interactions upon hover.

### Chips & Curation Selectors
- Used for wedding themes (e.g., *Tuscan Garden*, *Black Tie Champagne*, *Bohemian Romance*).
- Inactive: Pale blush ivory background (`#FDFBF7`), subtle rose border, `#594D46` body label.
- Active: `#F7D7CE` blush background with a crisp `#C5A059` micro-border and gold checkmark indicator.

### Input Fields & Monogram Customizers
- Floating label styling set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Default: `#FDFBF7` background, 1px `#E8DCD1` stroke.
- Focus: Delicate double ring effect—inner 1px `#C5A059` and outer 3px ambient blush aura (`rgba(247, 215, 206, 0.45)`). No abrupt high-contrast outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom-drawn 18px squares with 3px micro-corners. Checked state fills with `#C5A059` carrying an ivory checkmark. Radio buttons display an elegant outer gold ring surrounding a centered champagne dot.

### Specialized Brand Components
- **Wax Seal Badge**: Circular stamp element featuring metallic relief and deep shadow (`0 4px 10px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`), used for authenticity markers and signature hampers.
- **Satin Ribbon Step Progress**: Ribbon-like step bar navigating the customer through: 1. Vessel Selection, 2. Champagne & Treats, 3. Keepsake Engraving, 4. Hand-calligraphed Vows.
- **Gift Note Parchment Viewer**: Text area styled as deckle-edge handmade cream paper with italicized serif preview simulating live metallic calligraphy ink.
